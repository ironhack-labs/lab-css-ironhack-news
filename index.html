Learn DevOps and Cloud

The DevOps revolution has truly begun, with a drastic increase in the number of organizations adopting a DevOps culture. This means jobs, jobs, and more jobs! According to GitLab's 2023 Global DevSecOps Survey, 56% of organizations in 2023 are using DevOps or DevSecOps methodologies globally, up from 47% in 2022. Cloud is also on many company’s minds, with 71% running a quarter or more of their applications in the cloud. The number of roles for professionals with these skills are growing faster than the talent pool can rise to meet the demand, and we don’t see that trend going anywhere fast. Future proof your skill set, and get ready for a world of opportunities.
